Google Play Store Compliance

Account and Data Deletion

Application: Magic Diary • Developer: MahatoSoftware • Last Updated: October 24, 2026

At Magic Diary, we are committed to complete transparency and user data control. In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), this page outlines the steps you can take to request the permanent deletion of your Magic Diary account and all associated stored records.

1. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

We provide two convenient methods for deleting your account and records:

Method A: In-App Self-Service Deletion (Immediate)

If you have the Magic Diary app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly:

  1. Open the Magic Diary app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Profile & Settings from the navigation bar.
  3. Tap Account Settings.
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Verify your account credentials:
    • If registered via Email & Password: Enter your current password.
    • If registered via Google Sign-In: Verify with Google re-authentication.
  7. Check the confirmation box ("I understand that all my data will be permanently wiped") and tap Delete Account.

✓ Deletion is processed immediately. Your authentication credentials, Cloud Firestore records, and uploaded files are instantly purged.

Method B: Web / Email Deletion Request (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have access to the Magic Diary app or have uninstalled it from your device, you may submit a deletion request via email:

  • Email Address: mahatosoftware@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - Magic Diary
  • Required Details: Send the email from the same email address associated with your Magic Diary account.
  • Processing Timeline: Your request will be verified and permanently processed within 30 calendar days. You will receive a confirmation email once complete.

2. Data Types Deleted vs. Kept

Upon confirming an account deletion request (either in-app or via email), the following categories of data are handled:

Data Category Specific Data Elements Action Taken
Account & Authentication User ID (UID), Display Name, Registered Email Address, Username, Profile Photo URL, Firebase Auth credentials. Permanently Purged
Life & Diary Records Personal diary logs, journal entries, mood records, daily reflections, and personal memories. Permanently Purged
Financial & Household Records Bill accounts, due dates, expense tracking, payments, school fees, property details, and vehicle logs. Permanently Purged
Uploaded Media & Files Invoices, warranty receipts, captured photos, and PDF documents stored in Google Cloud Storage. Permanently Purged
Reminders & Device Tokens Scheduled alerts, reminder titles, notification settings, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens. Permanently Purged
✗ What Data Is Kept?

None. Magic Diary does not retain any user records, documents, transaction logs, or personal information following account deletion. All data is completely and irreversibly purged from our live Cloud Firestore databases and Cloud Storage buckets.

3. Additional Retention Period

There is no additional retention period:

  • In-app deletion: Records and cloud files are deleted immediately in real time.
  • Email requests: All records are deleted within 30 days of verification.
  • Backups: Any routine system database backups that may contain snapshots are automatically overwritten and purged in accordance with standard Google Cloud retention cycles.

4. Deleting Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

You do not need to delete your entire account to remove specific entries or documents. Magic Diary provides granular self-service deletion controls directly within each section:

  • Diary Entries: Swipe or tap the delete button on any individual diary entry to remove it.
  • Uploaded Documents & Receipts: Go to the Document Library or Warranty card to delete specific attachments or photos from Cloud Storage.
  • Bills & Expenses: Delete specific bill accounts, transactions, or property/vehicle maintenance records individually at any time.
  • Reminders: Dismiss or delete individual scheduled notifications from the Reminders screen.

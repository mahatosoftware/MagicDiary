1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Magic Diary, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

A. Account and Profile Information

  • Authentication Data: When you register or sign in via email or Google Sign-In, we collect your unique account identifier (User ID), email address, display name, and profile picture URL provided by your identity provider (Firebase Authentication / Google).
  • Credentials: Passwords are never stored on our servers; they are processed securely using Google Firebase Authentication.

B. User-Generated Life Records & Content

Magic Diary functions as your personal record-keeping ledger. The data you enter remains your property. Depending on the modules you use, this may include:

  • Daily Journal & Notes: Diary entries, moods, daily logs, reflections, and personal memories.
  • Financial & Bill Records: Bill due dates, expense logs, payment tracking, and school fee records. Note: Magic Diary is a record-keeping tool and does not process direct credit card or banking transactions.
  • Property & Vehicle Records: Asset descriptions, maintenance dates, renewal reminders, warranty expiry details, and associated metadata.
  • Reminders & Tasks: Notification titles, due dates, reminder notes, and scheduled alert frequencies.

C. Uploaded Media, Photos & Documents

  • Files and Attachments: Receipts, warranty cards, invoices, identification papers, and image attachments that you deliberately upload using the image picker or file picker. These files are securely hosted on Google Cloud Storage via Firebase Storage.

D. Device & Technical Usage Data

  • Device Identifiers: Operating system version, device model, app version, language preference, and unique device tokens (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens for notification delivery).
  • Diagnostic & Crash Logs: Performance data, crash reports, and system operational logs used strictly to troubleshoot and improve app reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate application purposes, including:

  • Providing Core Services: Authenticating your identity, syncing your records across devices via Cloud Firestore, and serving your uploaded documents.
  • Local & Push Notifications: Sending you time-sensitive reminders for upcoming bills, fee payments, warranties, and daily reflections.
  • Report & PDF Generation: Generating exportable PDF summaries and printable documents when requested by you.
  • Displaying Advertisements: Providing free access to our app through non-intrusive ad placements served by Google Mobile Ads.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting user accounts against unauthorized access, abuse, and technical vulnerabilities.
Commitment to Data Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal records, diaries, or uploaded documents to any third party or data broker. Your life logs are your own.

4. Advertising Policy (Google AdMob)

Magic Diary displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To display relevant ads or manage ad frequency, Google may collect and use:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA).
  • Approximate coarse location / IP address.
  • In-app interaction data and ad viewing metrics.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based ads anytime via your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID or Reset Advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > toggle off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”.

5. Storage, Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security safeguards:

  • Data Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device, Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS protocols.
  • Local Secure Storage: Sensitive device tokens and session state are secured on your device using hardware-backed keystores (Flutter Secure Storage / Keychain / KeyStore).
  • Access Controls: Firebase Security Rules are enforced at the database level so that only authenticated users can access their own diaries, bills, and documents.
  • Data Retention: We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or specific records, they are immediately removed from production databases.

6. Device Permissions

Magic Diary may request specific device permissions only when necessary for relevant features:

  • Photos & Storage (Read/Write): Required to attach photos to diary entries, upload receipts/warranty documents, and save generated PDF reports to your device.
  • Camera (Optional): Required if you choose to photograph documents or capture pictures directly within the app.
  • Notifications: Required to deliver timely alerts for upcoming bills, warranty expirations, and custom reminders.

You may grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device operating system settings.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you are entitled to exercise the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. You can also export summaries as PDF files directly in the app.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit or update your diary entries, records, and profile details anytime inside the application.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You have the absolute right to delete individual entries or request permanent deletion of your complete account and all associated cloud data.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to the processing of your data or opt out of personalized advertisements.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, deny services, or charge different prices for exercising any of your privacy rights.

8. Account & Data Deletion Instructions

How to Delete Your Account and Stored Records

We provide full self-service data deletion directly within the Magic Diary app:

  1. Open the Magic Diary app on your device.
  2. Go to Profile or Settings.
  3. Select Account Settings > Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your decision. Upon confirmation, your authentication account, Cloud Firestore entries, uploaded documents, and associated cloud backups are permanently purged.

Alternatively, you may email us directly at mahatosoftware@gmail.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request” from your registered email address, and we will delete your data within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Magic Diary is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at mahatosoftware@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete such information.
